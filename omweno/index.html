Mwamogesa Konyana Self Help Group

Constitution 2026

Section A: Preliminary Provisions

1. Establishment and Location

Mwamogesa Konyana Self Help Group is hereby established as a non-political, non-sectarian, voluntary self-help group. The organization is hereby located in Mwamogesa Village, Daraja Mbili Sub-Location, Kiamwasi Location, Kitutu Central Ward, Kisii County, near Daraja Mbili Market.

2. Address

Mwamogesa Konyana Self Help Group
P.O. Box 3238–40200
Kisii

3. Legal Compliance

The group shall operate in accordance with the laws of Kenya, including the Societies Act (Cap 108) and any other relevant legislation governing community-based organizations.

Section B: Purpose and Objectives

4. Vision

To create an enabling environment for enhancing poverty eradication through education and business, aimed at improving the living standards of the people within the area of operation.

5. Mission

To provide health care support for the aged and persons with disabilities, and to improve the educational and economic status of members and the community.

6. Core Objectives

  • To provide health care support for the aged and persons with disabilities.
  • To promote educational advancement and economic empowerment among members.
  • To foster financial, social, emotional, mental, and spiritual support among members.
  • To undertake community development projects including, but not limited to, funeral support.
  • To facilitate savings and credit activities (merry-go-round) for members.
  • To engage in any lawful activities that promote the welfare of members and the community.

7. Group Activities

  • Community projects (including funeral support).
  • Merry-go-round (savings and credit rotations).
  • Any other lawful activities as may be approved by the members.

Section C: Membership

8. Eligible Membership

To be eligible for membership, an individual must:

  • Be 18 years and above and willing to submit a copy of their National ID Card to the office.
  • Come from within the area of operation.
  • Be of sound mind and good character.
  • Be willing to pay a non-refundable registration fee of Ksh 6,000 (Six Thousand Kenya Shillings) to the group.
  • Be willing to pay an annual subscription fee of Ksh 200 for continued membership.
  • Members above 70 years shall produce their Identity Card to be exempted from regular contributions; however, those who are willing may contribute voluntarily.

9. Nature of Membership

Membership is open to all who meet the eligibility criteria, without discrimination based on tribe, religion, gender, or political affiliation.

10. Rights of a Member

Every member shall have the right to:

  • Give their opinion in a meeting.
  • Vote in group elections and on key resolutions.
  • Access information about the financial status of the group from the executive committee.

11. Duties of a Member

Every member shall:

  • Pay subscriptions, contributions, and fees as required.
  • Participate actively in group activities and meetings.
  • Abide by this constitution and any group regulations.
  • Promote the welfare and objectives of the group.
  • Treat fellow members with dignity and respect.
  • Report any misuse of group funds or resources to the executive committee.

12. Beneficiaries

The group's welfare programs, including funeral contributions, emergency assistance, and merry-go-round benefits, shall be extended strictly to the following categories:

  • Principal Contributors: Members who are fully paid up and maintain a nil balance (no outstanding arrears) with the group.
  • Spouses: Lawful spouses of principal contributors, provided the principal contributor maintains a nil balance.
  • Children: Biological or legally recognized children of principal contributors, below the age of 18 years, provided the principal contributor maintains a nil balance.
  • College/University Students: Children of principal contributors aged between 18 and 24 years who are enrolled in a recognized institution of higher learning. Such beneficiaries must present a valid student ID card as proof, and the principal contributor must maintain a nil balance.
  • Members Above 70 Years: To qualify for beneficiary status, any member above the age of 70 years must have a son or daughter who is an active, contributing member of the group.

13. Termination of Membership

Membership shall cease through:

  • Voluntary Resignation: Any member resigning shall give a three-month written notice to the Secretary. The resignation takes effect upon expiry of the notice, provided the member clears all outstanding debts and returns all group property.
  • Expulsion for Misconduct: A member may be expelled for gross misconduct that breaches this constitution. The member shall be given notice of the allegations and an opportunity to be heard by the disciplinary committee before any decision is made.
  • Death of a member.
  • Accumulated Arrears: Failure to contribute for three (3) consecutive scheduled contribution periods (without a valid reason accepted by the executive committee) shall lead to automatic termination.

14. Discipline of Members

  • The group shall establish a Disciplinary Committee of 3 to 5 members.
  • Penalties for offences shall include but not be limited to: late coming, absenteeism, drunkenness, disturbance, abusive language, and gross misconduct. Penalties shall be determined by the committee and ratified by the executive.

Section D: Office Bearers and Governance

15. Office Bearers

The group shall have the following office bearers (positions may be combined or expanded as needed):

  • Chairperson
  • Vice Chairperson
  • Secretary
  • Vice Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Female Representative
  • Organizing Secretary
  • Patron

16. Elections

  • Notice of elections shall be given 2 weeks to 1 month in advance to the Social Development Office and to all group members.
  • Elections must be supervised by the relevant authority unless the officer delegates the duty.
  • Members shall agree on the voting method (secret ballot, raising hands, or mlolongo technique).
  • The term of office shall be 2 years. An official may be re-elected for more than one term depending on performance.

Voting Eligibility

  • Only active members with nil balances may vote.
  • Members must present their ID to vote.
  • Voters must be physically present.
  • A two-thirds (2/3) quorum of members is required for elections to take place.

17. Removal of Office Bearers

An office bearer shall cease to hold office through:

  • Voluntary resignation (with 14 days' written notice to the Secretary).
  • Death.
  • Expiry of term.
  • Imprisonment for a period exceeding three (3) months.
  • Physical or mental incapacity to perform duties.
  • Misconduct including: embezzlement or misappropriation of group funds; gross violation of this constitution; criminal conviction; unethical practices that bring the group into disrepute.

18. Qualifications for Office Bearers

  • Must be an active member with a nil balance.
  • Must be literate (especially Secretary and Treasurer).
  • Must have a valid National ID card.
  • Must have a clean record (no criminal convictions).

19. Roles and Responsibilities of Office Bearers

Chairperson

  • Chair all group and executive committee meetings.
  • Invite members through the Secretary.
  • Serve as one of the bank signatories.
  • Provide general guidelines for the group's affairs.
  • Serve as the official spokesperson of the group.

Vice Chairperson

  • Act as Chairperson in their absence.
  • Perform duties delegated by the Chairperson.

Secretary

  • Keep and record meeting minutes.
  • Organize meeting venues.
  • Custodian of all non-financial records (excluding cash).
  • Serve as one of the bank signatories.
  • Handle all official group correspondence.
  • Custodian of the group assets register.

Vice Secretary

  • Assist the Secretary in their duties.
  • Act as Secretary in their absence.

Treasurer

  • Receive all money from various group activities.
  • Custodian of all financial records.
  • Serve as a bank signatory.
  • Provide financial reports and audited accounts during the AGM.

Organizing Secretary

  • Coordinate group activities and events.
  • Mobilize members for meetings and activities.

Male / Female Representatives

  • Represent the gender interests of the group.
  • Ensure gender balance in decision-making.

Patron

  • Provide guidance and oversight.
  • Act as an advisor to the executive committee.
  • Shall not be a voting member of the executive committee.

Section E: Financial Management

20. Sources of Group Funds

  • Member registration fees and annual subscription fees.
  • Donations from well-wishers and grants from donors.
  • Fundraising drives.
  • Investments and income-generating activities.

21. Group Funds Management

  • The group shall maintain an operational bank account in a reputable bank.
  • Three (3) signatories shall be appointed (Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer). Any withdrawal requires a minimum of two (2) signatures, one of whom must be either the Chairperson or the Treasurer.
  • Funds shall be utilized only on ventures agreed upon by the executive committee and approved by members in a meeting, with decisions minuted and signed.

22. Financial Controls and Approvals

  • The financial year shall run from 1st January to 31st December.
  • An annual budget shall be prepared and approved at the AGM.
  • Expenditure below Ksh 5,000 may be approved by the Treasurer and Chairperson.
  • Expenditure between Ksh 5,001 and Ksh 20,000 requires executive committee approval and must be minuted.
  • Any expenditure exceeding Ksh 20,000 shall require full member approval in a General Meeting.
  • A petty cash float not exceeding Ksh 5,000 shall be maintained by the Treasurer.
  • Audited accounts shall be presented at the AGM.

Section F: Meetings

23. Types of Meetings

  • Annual General Meeting (AGM): Held once every year. Notice accompanied by the annual statement of account and agenda shall be sent to members not less than 21 days before the date. Quorum: 50% of registered members.
  • Regular Meetings: Held to facilitate group activities (merry-go-rounds, funerals). Quorum: 30% of registered members.
  • Special General Meetings: Called by a notice of 7 days to deliberate on urgent matters (e.g., use of funds).
  • Executive Committee Meetings: Held as needed to discuss group affairs. Quorum: 50% of executive members.

24. Meeting Procedures

  • All meetings shall be chaired by the Chairperson or Vice Chairperson.
  • Decisions shall be made by a simple majority vote, unless otherwise specified. In case of a tie, the Chairperson shall have a casting vote.
  • Minutes shall be recorded, confirmed at the next meeting, and signed by the Chairperson and Secretary.

Section G: Records and Assets

25. Financial Records

  • Receipt book, Order book, Invoice book, Cash book, Petty cash voucher, and Bank transaction files.

26. Non-Financial Records

  • Minutes of meetings, Constitution, Members register, Visitors book, Strategic plan, Cards, and General correspondence files.

27. Group Assets

  • The group shall maintain a physical office within the location of operation.
  • A Group Assets Register shall be kept by the Secretary, detailing: description of asset, acquisition date, purchase price, condition, and estimated value.
  • An annual stocktaking shall be conducted.
  • A Finance and Procurement Committee shall oversee the purchase of group assets.
  • A Disposal and Sharing Committee shall oversee the sharing and disposal of assets.

Section H: Dispute Resolution

28. Dispute Resolution Procedure

  • Any dispute shall first be referred to the Dispute Resolution Committee appointed by the group.
  • The Committee shall have 14 days to mediate and report back.
  • If mediation fails, the matter shall be referred to the Executive Committee.
  • If unresolved, it shall be escalated to the Social Development Office, Department of Social Services, or to a court of law as a last resort.
  • No member shall seek external intervention without exhausting internal mechanisms.
  • A member aggrieved by a decision may appeal to the next General Meeting, whose decision shall be final.

Section I: Amendments, Dissolution & Indemnity

29. Amendment of the Constitution

  • Proposed amendments shall be submitted in writing to the Secretary at least 21 days before the meeting.
  • A two-thirds (2/3) quorum of members is required for amendment discussions.
  • The amendment must be approved and endorsed for adoption.
  • The amended constitution shall be submitted to the Social Development Officer for filing.

30. Dissolution

  • The group may be dissolved by a resolution passed by a 75% majority of all members at a Special General Meeting called for that purpose.
  • Upon dissolution, all debts and obligations shall be settled.
  • Any remaining assets shall be disposed of to a registered community-based organization with similar objectives. No assets shall be distributed to individual members.
  • The dissolution shall be registered with the relevant government authority.

31. Indemnity

An indemnity/acceptance form shall be signed by the elected executive and copied to the Social Development Officer.

Section J: Commitment Clause

32. Commitment

All members shall sign this constitution to confirm that they have read, contributed to, and fully understood the provisions herein. By signing, members commit to abide by this constitution and any lawful regulations passed by the group.

33. Effective Date

This constitution shall take effect immediately upon adoption by the members and registration with the relevant authorities.

Adoption and Signatories

Adopted by the members of Mwamogesa Konyana Self Help Group.

Signed by: Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and Patron.
